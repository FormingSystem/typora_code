## Interaction and Status Ownership

`reading_media_viewer` uniquely manages the viewer, zooming, displacement, keyboard focus, and resource release within the window. Mermaid continues to copy the already rendered SVG and rewrite the internal ID. The image only takes the actual resource address and natural size of the already loaded image, displaying it as an independent img, without modifying the original image's color, size, or file. The native chart and `reading_image_viewer` only provide the source and the content to open, with the unified entry responsible for the floating layer and button geometry; neither of them sets the top/right or hover style individually.

It continues to use the six controls of the existing Mermaid: zoom in, zoom out, adapt width, adapt screen, 100%, exit full screen; Ctrl + scroll wheel zooms around the pointer, left-click drag, double-click canvas to adapt screen, and Esc to exit. When the image is opened, it is fully adapted to the screen without changing the size of the large or small image, and 100% restores the natural size. The chart maintains its original 100% initial state. Manual zooming continues to use the 1.25 step, with a maximum of 600%, and allows less than 20% when adapting to extremely large images to ensure complete viewing. The viewer uses the current reading background, and the image itself does not perform theme inversion.

The narrow window toolbar wraps to a new line, and the canvas follows the remaining space; Tab is limited to the viewer, and exit according to [R032](workspace_interaction.en.md#section_6de9c37eb80b) to restore the editing focus or selection before the opening; keyboard starts from the entry and returns the entry, maintaining the reading scroll. Ctrl shortcuts do not penetrate into window zooming or document editing. Close the viewer when switching sources, source resource changes, reader destruction, or enhancement unloading; asynchronous image loading failures provide a closable error message, and do not retain invalid zoom buttons.
